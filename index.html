🐕 hachiko-usage - Track your Claude token usage today

Download Hachiko for Windows

Hachiko is a small pet that lives on your Windows taskbar. It shows your Claude token usage in a thought bubble. You see how many tokens you use during your current session and your remaining weekly limit.

✨ What the app does

Managing API limits often takes too much effort. Hachiko removes this friction. The pet sits at the bottom of your screen and provides updates as you work. You do not need to open complex dashboards to check your stats. The visual bubble keeps your usage front and center.

🚀 How to install

Follow these steps to set up the software on your computer:

  1. Visit the official releases page.
  2. Locate the latest installer file ending in .exe.
  3. Click the file to download it to your Downloads folder.
  4. Open the installer and follow the instructions on your screen.
  5. Launch the application from your Start menu once the process ends.

⚙️ System requirements

Hachiko works on any standard computer running Windows 10 or Windows 11. It uses very little memory and will not slow down your machine. Ensure your internet connection stays active to receive real-time usage data from Claude.

📋 How to use

After you start the application, look for a small icon near your system clock. Hachiko will appear shortly after launch. If you need to refresh your data, right-click the pet and select the refresh option. The thought bubble updates automatically when you send requests to the API.

🏠 Privacy and technical details

This software does not store your private API keys on remote servers. All your data stays on your local machine. The tool connects only to official endpoints to fetch your current consumption metrics. We value the safety of your information.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

If the pet does not appear, check your taskbar settings to ensure the app is not hidden. If the numbers do not update, verify your API key is correctly entered in the settings menu. Most issues resolve after a simple restart of the application.
